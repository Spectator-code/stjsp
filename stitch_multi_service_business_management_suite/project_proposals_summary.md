# Capstone / Project Proposals Summary
Presented by: NAMI MIGUEL

The submitted text contains 4 distinct system project proposals:

1. **St. Joseph Driving School Management & Scheduling System**
   - Centralized management of students, courses, instructors, vehicles, training schedules, progress tracking, and payments.
   - Client: St. Joseph Driving School.
   - Scope: Student & Enrollment, Instructor/Vehicle scheduling, progress monitoring, payment recording.

2. **Tables and Chairs Reservation and Inventory Management System (Rental)**
   - Web-based equipment rental and inventory tracking.
   - Client: University of Mindanao – Tagum City.
   - Scope: Customer management, stock/quantity/condition tracking, reservations, release & return logs, damage/loss recording.

3. **Memorial Service Management and Reservation System**
   - Web-based memorial arrangement, scheduling, package, and reservation system.
   - Client: Memorial Service Provider (Tagum City).
   - Scope: Client records, package catalog, scheduling, record retrieval, reporting.

4. **FixPoint: Computer & Mobile Device Repair Management System**
   - End-to-end device service workflow: Customer -> Device Intake -> Diagnosis -> Quotation -> Technician Assignment -> Parts & Repair -> QC/Payment -> Release & History.
   - Client: FixPoint Computer & Mobile Repair.
   - Scope: Repair tickets, parts inventory, quotations & billing, technician tracking, customer status portal.
